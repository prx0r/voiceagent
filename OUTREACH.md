# Outreach kit — electricians, Manchester first (prospects_electrical.csv)

## Email (send Tue-Thu 7-9am)
Subject: 3 of your reviews mention no callback

Hi [FIRST],

Checked [BUSINESS]'s Google reviews — [N] mention customers not getting
through. That's the most common 1-star trigger for sparkies round here.

We answer missed calls in your own voice, text you the urgent ones, and
email the rest by 7am. One divert code, set up on a 10-min call. First 10
calls free, no subscription — 50p a minute only when it answers.

Reply YES and our AI will ring you for 60 seconds. Pretend to be your own
worst customer.

[YOUR NAME] — [DEMO NUMBER: ring it now, that's the AI]

## On YES (auto)
1. TPS screen the number (crm.tps_check — sole traders need this consent, they just gave it).
2. AI demo call within 1 working hour, 90 seconds max:
   - disclose + permission ("cold call, 60 seconds, shall I go on?")
   - role-flip: they play their worst customer, agent runs intake
   - live SMS mid-call with the intake card
   - close: Thursday setup call + divert code, or demo-line ring tonight
3. Log outcome (booked / demo-line / dead) for the morning review.

## Rules
- Email businesses only where a public listing exists; one follow-up max.
- Sole traders: never AI-call without the YES. Ltds: TPS-screen first.
- Every touch logged; opt-outs honored same day, list cleaned same day.
- Track: touches -> YES rate -> demo completions -> trials -> paying.
  Kill: 200 touches, <5 trials = message wrong. Trials stalling = demo quality.
