## The gap audit (personalise per prospect before sending)
Check 4 things, name what's missing:
1. GBP claimed + hours + 24/7 flag + photos? (feeds 54% of AI answers)
2. Checkatrade / TrustATrader / trade body (NICEIC/Gas Safe) present?
3. Reviews: count, recency, replies? (below 4.0 stars = near-invisible to AI)
4. After-hours: what happens at 9pm Saturday? (voicemail = the leak)
