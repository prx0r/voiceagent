## Consensus patterns (steal all six)
1. Live-source-only answers + explicit handoff when unsure (everyone; = our graph+guard).
2. WhatsApp = money channel (initiated msgs work); Instagram = discovery only.
3. One config/record everywhere; Shopify (or our case file) stays source of truth.
4. Pre-live playground/evals before customers arrive (= our eval_text.py).
5. WA fees at cost, flat platform fee (bitbybit) — copies well to sparkies.
6. Setup ≤30 min, no code; Embedded Signup for WA number.

## Gaps none of them cover (our wedge)
Voice/PSTN calls, services calendar, per-job worth scoring, trade compliance (certs/DNO),
after-hours wake logic, photo→quote ranges, job feed. All six are retail/Shopify-shaped.
Nobody owns the van.
