# ShopyAgent (Mukh.one) — brief (source: shopyagent.mukh1.com, fetched 2026-09-10)

Positioning: cheapest path to live; agency-first (rebill at margin).

## What it does
- Storefront widget + same brain on WhatsApp/IG/Messenger; interactive product cards.
- Live-catalog-only answers ("never invents"), own guardrail engine vs prompt injection
  (explicitly NOT a bolt-on — they market the guardrail itself, like our guard.py).
- Zero-prompt onboarding: auto-builds KB from site (policies/FAQs/sizing), detects brand
  theme, sandbox preview. Forgiving typo-tolerant search → cart → store checkout or COD.
- No-website mode: WhatsApp-as-store menu mode.
- Agency model: shared reply pool across client stores, per-store branding/usage, client
  logins hide margins.

## Pricing
Per-REPLY (one AI-answered message; button taps/cart ops free). Hard-capped plans + reply
packs. Free to build/preview, pay on go-live, cancel anytime. (Exact tiers behind login.)

## Tech pattern to steal
Reply-based billing (cleaner than seats for chat); theme-app-embed install; menu-mode
store for sellers with no site (= our sparky with no website); guardrails as a marketed
feature; agency pool = template for our multi-sparky rollout (one kernel, per-tradie brand).
