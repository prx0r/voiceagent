# Whoza — primary UK rival (sources: whoza.ai + /pricing + /for-electricians, 2026-09-10)

Scotland-built. Voice agent "Katie" (12 UK voices) + Mark; review engine "Claire";
competitor/AI-visibility "Rex". Live with plumbers/electricians/builders/roofers.

## Pricing (the anchor to beat)
£59 (10 jobs, 100 min) / £125 (20, 300) / £230 (40, 700) / £399 (100, 1500);
overage £0.40/min; extra jobs £4.50→£2.25 sliding. 7-day trial, no contract.
Sparky math they sell: one £150-300 callout or one EICR pays the year.

## What they do well (steal the patterns)
- RED/AMBER/GREEN urgency flags on every call (= our wake/routine, marketed better).
- WhatsApp job card with one-tap Accept/Call-back/Decline; only ACCEPTED jobs billed
  (= our idempotency + case actions, plus billing fairness worth copying).
- Sparky qualification script: postcode, job type (EICR/fault/rewire/consumer unit/EV),
  urgency, property type, landlord-vs-homeowner, EICR property size/circuits, rewire
  value flag (£2-8k → priority). High-value poaching target for our survey flow.
- Live mid-call transfer; voicemail detect + urgent-flag; spam filter; calendar sync
  (Google/Outlook/Apple); review auto-requests; Jobber/ServiceTitan CRM (Pro);
  searchable transcripts (30d/90d/1yr retention ladder — note: ours has no stated cap).
- Free tools as acquisition (missed-call calculator, rate checker, callout uplift tool).

## Gaps (our openings)
No worth-it scoring, no parts, no cert/DNO depth, no job feed, no IT wedge, no
open API for others to build on. Answering is the commodity; everything after is open.
