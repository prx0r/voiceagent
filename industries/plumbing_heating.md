# Plumbing & heating (UK) — adjacent trade report

Why here: same kernel, same after-hours economics (leaks/boiler failures are the
plumbing equivalent of burning smells), same admin pain. Second trade after
electrical proves the vertical is portable. Gas work is mentioned only to fence it.

## Legislation that shapes the product

| Law / instrument | Requirement | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| Gas Safety (Installation and Use) Regulations 1998 | Gas work ONLY by Gas Safe registered engineers; annual landlord gas safety checks (CP12) | HARD FENCE: agent never books/triages gas work to non-Gas-Safe engineers; gas smell → evacuate + National Gas Emergency (0800 111 999), not us. Mirrors electrical safety escalation. |
| Building Regulations Part L (fuel/power conservation), Part G (sanitation/hot water), Part J (combustion appliances) | Efficiency + safety standards for installs | Quote templates reference current Parts; heat-pump jobs cite MCS + Boiler Upgrade Scheme eligibility (never guaranteed). |
| Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations 1999 (+ WRAS approvals) | Fittings must not contaminate/waste; notification for certain work | Survey asks about unvented cylinders/water softeners routing; approved-fittings line in quotes. |
| Legionella control (HSE L8/HSG274) | Landlords/employers manage legionella risk in water systems | Landlord flow: annual checks reminder, same engine as EICR reminders. |
| Consumer Rights Act 2015 (as electrical.md) | Care/skill, repeat-or-discount remedies | Same quote-validity + written-scope discipline. |

## Demand drivers
Boiler breakdowns (winter peaks = after-hours premium), landlord gas certificates
(annual, recurring), heat-pump installs (subsidised, survey-heavy — EV-charger shaped),
leak emergencies (triage: isolate stopcock → morning visit vs now).
