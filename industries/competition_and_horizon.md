# Competition & horizon: UK, US, and what's coming (2026-09-10, web-verified)

## UK direct rivals (AI receptionists for trades — crowded £20-60 end)
| Rival | Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Whoza (Scotland) | £59-399/mo + per-job | Closest to us: RED/AMBER/GREEN triage, WhatsApp delivery, calendar sync, Jobber/ServiceTitan CRM, review engine, sparky-specific qualification (EICR/rewire/fault). The one to beat. |
| tradereceptionist | £49-249/mo | Qualify + SMS/email + calendar booking, price-talk toggle. |
| Callio | £20-100/mo | UK accents/terminology, transcripts. Price floor. |
| team-connect | £10-80/mo | Cheapest; emergency routing + webhooks even at low end. |
| liorra | ~£199/mo | Missed-call-only mode (rings 4-6x first) — clever adoption trick worth copying. |
| bobsbots | £399-499/mo | Premium emergency niche (plumbers/locksmiths), WhatsApp paid jobs. |

Implication: answering calls is commoditising. Our edge must be everything AFTER the
answer: worth-it scoring, parts, certs/DNO depth, job feed, IT second wedge. Nobody
above does any of that. Copy liorra's missed-call-only mode for onboarding.

## US playbook (what's winning, what to steal)
- Avoca: $125M Series B Apr 2026 @ $1B (!), 800+ shops, ServiceTitan Gold. Proves
  vertical depth + FSM integration = unicorn path. Overkill below ~$3M revenue.
- Sameday: 2M+ calls, ~$1.25B customer revenue, flat unlimited pricing. Proves flat
  pricing wins SMB trust.
- Hatch: 2,000+ customers, $500-2500/mo multi-channel (voice+SMS+email with shared
  context). Proves channels converge — our kernel already does.
- ServiceTitan Max (agentic OS): +500bps booking, +1000bps close, locations doubled
  in a quarter. Proves incumbents bundle AI free — standalone must win on depth.
- Goodcall's missing emergency dispatch is "disqualifying" per reviewers — triage is
  THE differentiator. Our wake/queue logic is the crown jewel; price it like one.
- Budget end (Rosie $49, Upfirst $24.95) + Jobber $99 add-on: SMB price gravity.
  Adoption: 40%+ of $500K+ shops use AI (2023: 18%); 88% report more bookings in 30d.

## UK FSM incumbents (integrate, don't fight)
Tradify (£29-37, 2,700 reviews — SMB default), Powered Now (£15-32, UK-native certs),
Jobber (USD billing, weak UK fit), Commusoft (£89+, gas/HVAC depth), Joblogic,
BigChange, simPRO. ServiceTitan UK weak (USD, $15-200K onboarding). Integration
targets: Tradify + Powered Now APIs first (where our sparkies live), Commusoft for
mid-market, Jobber open API as backstop.

## Legislation horizon (verified, all 2026)
1. **EICR Amendment Regs 2026** (in force 30 Nov 2026): standard moves to BS 7671:
   2018+A4:2026; pre-tenancy test duties clarified. → Re-inspection wave + graph update.
2. **Renters' Rights Act 2025** (Royal Assent Oct 2025; periodic tenancies from 1 May
   2026; PRS database late 2026; EPC C by 2030; Decent Homes 2035; Awaab's Law
   enforcement): landlord compliance workload explodes → EICR/remedial/agent demand.
3. **Cyber Security & Resilience Bill** (Lords 2nd reading Jul 2026): brings **MSPs,
   data centres, load controllers** into NIS scope + incident reporting + SoS powers.
   → Every UK MSP becomes a regulated entity: runbooks, evidence,SLA proof — our audit
   exports become a compliance product, not a nice-to-have. Biggest tailwind in this file.
4. Social-sector EICR duties (Nov 2025/2026 deadlines): housing-association portfolios
   need volume EICR capacity now.

## Strategy in one paragraph
Race Whoza on triage depth, not call answering; undercut the £59 anchor with ~£7
costs; integrate Tradify/Powered Now instead of replacing them; ride the 2026
regulatory wave (EICR amendment + Renters' Rights + NIS-for-MSPs) as demand gen;
keep the US lesson front and centre — emergency triage is the product, everything
else is plumbing.
