# Electrical contractors (UK) — industry report

Snapshot: ~£420 avg job, 38 calls/wk, 34% missed ≈ £1,950/wk leaking (see UK_MARKET.md).
Recurring compliance-driven demand (EICRs, remedials) + emergency callouts. Our wedge:
after-hours triage + survey booking + cert delivery in chat.

## Legislation that shapes the product

| Law / instrument | Requirement | Product implication |
|---|---|---|
| Electricity at Work Regulations 1989 | Work must be safe; competence required; no live working without justification | Agent NEVER diagnoses-then-dispatches as safe; safety signals → power-off advice + escalate. Refusal class in tools. |
| Building Regulations 2010, Part P (England & Wales) | Domestic electrical work notifiable unless by registered competent person / checked | Quote drafts must state Part P notification included; never promise sign-off by unregistered installer. |
| BS 7671 (IET Wiring Regulations; BS 7671:2018+A4:2026 "Orange Book" sole edition from 15 Oct 2026; EAS individual competence enforced 1 Oct 2026; new battery-storage chapter, PoE s716, photo/thermal evidence recognised) | The technical standard EICRs/certification judge against | Graph cites current edition + date; EICR-template refresh + battery/PoE survey questions are demand (see competition_and_horizon.md). Agent quotes ranges, never declares compliance. |
| Electrical Safety Standards in the Private Rented Sector (England) Regulations 2020 (SI 2020/312); extended to social sector Nov 2025 | 5-yearly inspection by qualified person; report to tenant in 28 days; remedial in 28 days; council can fine/arrange works | Recurring-revenue engine: landlord EICR reminders + remedial booking flow. Agent tracks due dates per property. |
| Competent Person Schemes (NICEIC, NAPIT, ELECSA) | Self-certification route for Part P | Tradie profile stores scheme ID; cert templates reference it; never invent. |
| Consumer Rights Act 2015 | Services with reasonable care/skill; repeat or price reduction on failure | Quote validity windows + written scope in every draft; case file = evidence. |
| Construction (Design and Management) Regulations 2015 | Duties on larger/notifiable construction work | Flag multi-trade/site jobs for human scoping; agent does domestic/small works only. |
| Waste Electrical and Electronic Equipment (WEEE) Regs 2013 | Correct disposal of electrical waste | Old-board swaps include disposal line in quote template. |

## Demand drivers to aim the funnel at
EICR due-date reminders (landlords), consumer-unit upgrades (old fuse boxes), fault
triage at night, EV chargers (see ev_charging.md). Emergency response 60-90 min is the
market norm to beat on speed-of-answer, not vans.
